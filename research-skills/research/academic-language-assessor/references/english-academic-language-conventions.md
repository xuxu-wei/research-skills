# English Academic Language Conventions

Use this reference when `target_language: English` or `bilingual`.

## Priorities

- Prefer precise, concrete verbs over vague verbs such as "get", "make", or "do".
- Preserve scientific caution without repetitive hedging.
- Avoid promotional or journalistic wording unless the evidence directly supports the claim.
- Keep Methods and Results direct, factual, and non-narrative.
- Keep claim strength aligned with study design and evidence status.

## Common Checks

- Article use, prepositions, subject-verb agreement, and modifier placement.
- Tense discipline conventions from `discipline-language-conventions.md`.
- Terminology consistency across abstract, main text, tables, and supplements.
- Sentence length and paragraph flow.
- Non-native English transfer patterns from `common-l1-interference-patterns.md`, flagged descriptively rather than judgmentally.
