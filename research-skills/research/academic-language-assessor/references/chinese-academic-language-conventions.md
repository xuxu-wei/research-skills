# Chinese Academic Language Conventions

Use this reference when `target_language: Chinese` or `bilingual`.

## Core Principle

Chinese academic polishing should make text **简洁、清晰、明确**. The assessor should flag wording for deletion when it adds decoration, defensiveness, or ambiguity without adding evidence or precision.

## Deletion-First Checks

- Delete redundant setup phrases such as "值得注意的是", "在一定程度上", "从某种意义上说" when they do not change the claim.
- Delete unnecessary metaphors, literary imagery, emotional descriptors, and decorative analogies.
- Delete stacked modifiers that make the noun phrase heavy without adding testable meaning.
- Delete repeated defensive caveats when one precise limitation is enough.
- Delete vague self-protective language such as "可能在某些方面具有一定的潜在价值" unless each qualifier is needed.
- Delete unsupported promotional claims such as "重大突破", "显著提升", "深刻揭示", "填补空白" unless the evidence and design support that strength.

## Clarity Checks

- Identify the sentence subject, action, object, comparison, population, time frame, and evidence status.
- Split overlong sentences when multiple claims, conditions, or caveats compete.
- Keep causal language distinct from association, prediction, description, and hypothesis generation.
- Make terms consistent between Chinese labels, English terms, abbreviations, and statistical endpoints.

## Mixed Chinese-English Formatting

- Keep English abbreviations, units, gene/protein names, database names, and method names consistent.
- Use consistent Chinese punctuation around English terms and numerals.
- Avoid switching between translated and untranslated terms unless both forms are defined.
