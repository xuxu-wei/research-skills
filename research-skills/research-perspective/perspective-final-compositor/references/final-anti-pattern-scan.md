# Final Anti-Pattern Scan

Before declaring readiness, scan for:

- new unregistered claim introduced during revision
- unsupported central claim
- absolute language inconsistent with claim strength
- caveat creep
- mini-review drift
- review-response language in body
- orphan paragraphs
- generic-profile submission overclaim
- title or abstract promising more than manuscript supports

Substantive findings must trigger a return route, not a compositor rewrite.
