# Style Guidelines

- Open with a concrete argumentative anchor, not generic field background.
- Every paragraph must advance one argument step.
- Prefer selective evidence over exhaustive literature summary.
- Keep section headings functional and specific.
- Remove sentences that only announce structure.
- Do not write review-response language in the manuscript body.
- Do not introduce a claim that is absent from `claim-ledger.md`.
- Keep limitations tied to the thesis; do not append generic caveat lists.

Paragraph map fields: paragraph number, function, argument step, Claim IDs, Evidence IDs, risks.
