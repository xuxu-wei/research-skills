# Narrative Strategies

## Straight-Line

Use when the thesis is already clear and the field tension is direct.
Structure: problem -> evidence mismatch -> interpretation -> implication.

## Contrastive

Use when the article turns on replacing a dominant view.
Structure: dominant view -> why it fails -> better frame -> consequences.

## Diagnostic

Use when the article explains why a recurring problem persists.
Structure: symptom -> mistaken explanation -> deeper cause -> corrective agenda.

## Agenda-Setting

Use when evidence is incomplete but the perspective can define the next question.
Structure: current uncertainty -> why it matters now -> priorities -> boundaries.

Do not switch strategies mid-draft unless the argument skeleton explicitly calls for it.
