# Citation Risk Checklist

Check every citation against the sentence it supports.

- Unsupported: citation does not support the proposition.
- Overextended: cited population, intervention, outcome, or context differs materially.
- Secondary-only: citation is a review or commentary when primary evidence is needed.
- Outdated: older source is used despite newer contrary or stronger evidence.
- Single-study overclaim: one study is used for a broad field-level claim.
- Method limitation hidden: major limitation is omitted.
- Illustrative-only evidence used as support.
- Guideline mismatch: outlet or clinical guideline is cited outside its intended scope.
- Retraction/correction risk not checked when high stakes.

Required output fields: citation ID, supported sentence, risk type, severity, required action.
