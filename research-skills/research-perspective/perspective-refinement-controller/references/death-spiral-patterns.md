# Death Spiral Patterns

Flag death spiral when at least two appear across consecutive revisions:

- caveats increase while hard-gate scores do not improve
- thesis becomes narrower but not more novel
- background expands to answer criticism instead of sharpening argument
- new claims appear to patch old weaknesses
- response-to-reviewers becomes stronger than the manuscript body
- evidence gaps are hidden by weaker language rather than routed to curator

Required response:
1. Stop normal drafting.
2. Identify the failing claim or argument step.
3. Choose `narrow_thesis`, `downgrade_claim_strength`, `split_claim`, `evidence_rebuild`, or `stop_no_gain`.
4. Record the decision in `decision-log.md`.
