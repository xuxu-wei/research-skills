- `previous_proposal_file_path`
- `updated_proposal_file_path`
- `previous_proposal_version`
- `updated_proposal_version`
- `revision_plan_summary`
- `must_fix_items_addressed`
- `must_fix_items_unresolved`
- `should_fix_items_addressed`
- `new_issues_introduced`
- `changes_to_research_question_or_aims`
- `changes_to_methods_or_feasibility_claims`
- `changes_to_novelty_or_impact_claims`
- `new_assumptions_requiring_confirmation`
- `recommended_next_step`
