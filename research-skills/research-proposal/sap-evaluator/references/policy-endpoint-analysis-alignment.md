# Endpoint-Analysis Alignment Policy

This policy guides evaluation of whether endpoints, metrics, analysis populations, and primary analyses are mutually consistent.

## Checkpoints

- Endpoint or metric is explicitly defined.
- Measurement timing and scale are specified when relevant.
- Analysis population corresponds to the research question.
- Primary analysis estimates the intended effect, association, prediction, diagnostic accuracy, comparison, or metric.
- Comparator, exposure, intervention, predictor, grouping variable, or target condition is defined when relevant.
- Covariates and adjustment strategy are aligned with design and causal or predictive goal.
- Interpretation of primary analysis matches the stated objective.

## Common Problems

- Endpoint is described clinically or conceptually but not analytically.
- Primary model targets a different question from the proposal.
- Analysis population excludes or includes participants inconsistent with the objective.
- Secondary analyses are overdeveloped while primary analysis is weak.
- Estimand or interpretation is ambiguous.
