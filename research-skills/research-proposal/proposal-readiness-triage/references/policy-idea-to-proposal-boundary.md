# Idea-to-Proposal Boundary Policy

This policy distinguishes an idea that should enter proposal drafting from one that should return to idea development.

## Proposal-Ready Idea

A proposal-ready idea has enough specificity to support a coherent proposal draft without inventing core facts.

It normally includes:

- a research question or objective;
- a study object;
- a value claim;
- an intended output;
- a plausible data or evidence path;
- an evaluable endpoint, outcome, metric, or deliverable;
- a plausible method or design route;
- known constraints or enough context to identify them.

## Idea Still Needs Refinement

Route to `needs_idea_refinement` when the input requires one or more of:

- generating candidate ideas;
- narrowing an overly broad topic;
- selecting among competing directions;
- constructing the research question;
- identifying a real gap;
- defining the study object;
- defining endpoint, outcome, metric, or deliverable;
- finding a feasible data or evidence path;
- merging or separating multiple ideas.

## Clarification vs Refinement

Use `needs_clarification` when a small number of factual answers from the user can unblock drafting.

Use `needs_idea_refinement` when the work requires conceptual development, idea comparison, re-scoping, or opportunity mapping.

## Methodology Preflight Boundary

Use `needs_methodology_preflight` when the idea is conceptually mature enough to be considered, but a specialist check is needed before drafting or before an explicitly requested SAP branch.

Typical triggers:

- uncertain data-method fit;
- unclear endpoint-analysis alignment;
- confounding or bias may invalidate the design;
- feasibility depends on method choice;
- SAP was requested and the analysis route is not yet safe to draft.

## Do Not Draft Through Uncertainty

Do not mark an idea ready if doing so would require the drafter to invent:

- primary endpoint;
- study population or dataset;
- data access;
- analytic method;
- intervention or exposure;
- outcome definition;
- feasibility constraints;
- user goal.
