# Supplementary Materials Guide

Supplementary materials are first-class artifacts.

## Rules

- Every supplementary item must appear in the Supplementary Index.
- Every supplementary item must be referenced from the main text or explicitly justified.
- Do not bury primary evidence only in supplementary materials.
- Supplementary methods must be independently understandable.
- Version numbers must match the paired manuscript version.
- Missing planned items are flagged, not fabricated.
