# Section Templates

## Default IMRaD Order

Draft Methods, Results, Introduction, then Discussion.

## Methods Minimum

Study design; setting; participants/samples; intervention/exposure; outcome definitions; statistical analysis; ethics/governance; reporting standard notes.

## Results Minimum

Participant/sample flow; descriptive foundation; primary result; secondary results; sensitivity/robustness; adverse events or negative findings when applicable.

## Introduction Minimum

Known context; evidence gap; objective/hypothesis.

## Discussion Minimum

Principal finding; comparison with literature; interpretation; strengths/limitations; implications; conclusion.

Follow the study-specific reporting standard selected in the context brief.
