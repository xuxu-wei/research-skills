# Overclaim Patterns

- Causal wording from observational data without causal design or analysis.
- "First", "novel", or "unique" without literature grounding.
- Generalizing beyond the sampled population.
- Treating exploratory subgroup results as confirmatory.
- Claiming equivalence from non-significant results without equivalence design.
- Hiding uncertainty by reporting only p-values or direction of effect.
- Making practice-changing claims from preliminary or underpowered evidence.

## Fixability

- Fixable: the claim can be softened, moved to Discussion, removed without collapsing the manuscript, or relinked to existing evidence.
- Unfixable: the primary claim has no evidence, contradicts the supplied evidence, requires new analysis/data collection, or would erase the manuscript's core contribution if corrected.
