# Delta Report Standards

A valid delta report states what changed, where, why, and which concern it addresses.

## Invalid Delta Language

- "Improved clarity" without location.
- "Polished wording" without examples or affected section.
- "Addressed reviewer comment" without mapping to concern ID.

## Required Sections

- Previous and updated manuscript versions.
- Addressed, partially addressed, and not addressed concerns.
- New issues introduced.
- Substantive changes to methods, results, primary claim, or contribution.
- Recommended next step.
