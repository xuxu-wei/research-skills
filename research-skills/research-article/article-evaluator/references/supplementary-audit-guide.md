# Supplementary Audit Guide

Check whether supplementary materials support rather than hide the manuscript.

## Checks

- Critical evidence buried only in supplementary.
- Main text references missing supplementary items.
- Supplementary items not referenced or justified.
- Supplementary material duplicates the main manuscript excessively.
- Journal limits and file format requirements.
- Data/code availability statements.

Any missing item tied to a primary claim is a major or critical issue.
