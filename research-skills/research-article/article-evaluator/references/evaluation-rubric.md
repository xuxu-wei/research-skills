# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension 1-10 and assign `pass`, `borderline`, or `fail`.

## Dimensions

- Scientific Validity
- Evidence-Claim Alignment
- Reporting Completeness
- Journal Fit
- Clarity & Structure
- Language & Academic Register
- Contribution Significance

## Anchors

- 9-10: submission-ready for the target venue.
- 7-8: strong with minor fixable issues.
- 5-6: major revision needed.
- 3-4: weak; substantial repair needed.
- 1-2: not ready or fundamentally flawed.

Non-compensatory gates override average score.
