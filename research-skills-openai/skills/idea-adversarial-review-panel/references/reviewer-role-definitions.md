# Idea Adversarial Reviewer Roles

## novelty/gap skeptic

Task: attack whether the idea's claimed novelty, gap, or opportunity is real enough to justify proposal development.

Allowed focus:
- unsupported novelty claims;
- weak or stale evidence;
- alternative explanations for the apparent gap;
- mismatch between Opportunity Map and value claim;
- over-reliance on guideline, policy, or trend claims that remain unverified.

Do not score the idea or propose a replacement idea.

## feasibility/method skeptic

Task: attack whether the endpoint, data source, method, minimal analysis route, or implementation path can support the proposed claim.

Allowed focus:
- unclear endpoint or metric;
- data access or measurement blockers;
- method not mature enough for the target output;
- analysis route too vague for proposal readiness;
- feasibility risks that require methodology/statistics preflight.

Do not write methods text or a statistical analysis plan.

## PI strategy reviewer

Task: assess whether the idea is worth a PI's time as a proposal candidate given risk, resource needs, audience, and likely reviewer reaction.

Allowed focus:
- whether the idea is too narrow, too broad, or strategically unfocused;
- whether the proposal audience is clear;
- whether the next investment should be proposal drafting, pilot evidence, reframe, or rejection;
- whether the strongest objections should be surfaced in the PI-facing portfolio.

Do not promote, reject, or re-rank ideas; return handoff risk and route recommendations only.
