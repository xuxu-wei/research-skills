# Evidence Limitation Rules

Judge only evidence represented inside the dossier. Do not open references or
maps and do not fill gaps from memory.

- Mark novelty/value support unverified or score conservatively when references,
  closest-work comparison, or evidence-chain support is absent, single-source,
  conflicting, or explicitly limited.
- For clinical/guideline claims, require the dossier to document current or
  user-provided evidence and its limitations.
- Similar prior work may still support replication, validation, application,
  translation, integration, resource, benchmark, or broader-audience value.
  Reject only unsupported novelty/impact claims, not honest repositioning.
- A qualified claim passes only while its necessary qualifier remains in the
  title, summary, and positioning.
