# Evidence Limitation Rules

## General Rule

Evaluation must stay within evidence boundaries.

## Unverified Novelty

Mark novelty as unverified or score conservatively when:

- evidence map is absent
- evidence is single-source
- claim comes only from a research article introduction or discussion
- citation search was not performed
- guideline alignment is unknown

## Clinical Ideas

Clinical ideas require web evidence or user-provided evidence. Without it, novelty and guideline alignment must remain unverified.
