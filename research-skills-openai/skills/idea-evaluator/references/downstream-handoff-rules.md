# Downstream Handoff Rules

Return the sealed report, logical dossier reference, chain/claim checks, scores,
gates, findings, decision, repairs, limitations, and post-freeze journal match
to the orchestrator.

The orchestrator may provide repair directions to the writer only after review
completion. Do not send a rewritten Idea. Method insufficiency may route to
preflight, but its findings must be incorporated into a new complete dossier
before a new fresh evaluator runs.

The assembler receives the sealed report and qualifying dossier pointer after
the orchestrator validates the current artifact ID/version/path and state.

Journal matching never changes promotion or repair routing. For every final
biomedical or clinical Idea, the orchestrator must give a fresh
`medical-journal-review` only the same dossier reference and score-free
`candidate_brief`, by value or as a separate logical artifact. Other domains
record `not_applicable`. Do not expose this evaluation report, scores, gates,
decision, findings, or repair directions to that reviewer.
