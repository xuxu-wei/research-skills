# Review considerations

Use these questions to focus a scientific assessment, not as a required report outline.

- **Question and contribution:** Is the uncertainty substantive? Would answering it change understanding, practice, a method, or an available resource? Is the claim of novelty supported at the scope stated?
- **Evidence:** Does the cited work support the gap and rationale? Are conflicting results or strong alternative explanations considered?
- **Design:** Can the proposed observations and comparisons answer the question? Are the inference and intended claim compatible?
- **Feasibility:** Are the data, measurements, time, expertise, and access plausible? Which assumptions are verified and which still need checking?
- **Value under uncertainty:** Would a null result, failed hypothesis, or limited effect still teach something useful? Is there a smaller informative first step?
- **Communication:** Can a researcher understand the idea and why it matters without reconstructing unstated assumptions?

An unidentifiable effect, inaccessible essential data, or a method that cannot measure the target can invalidate the current design. A missing citation, unclear explanation, or uncertain parameter may instead be repairable. Explain the distinction in context.
