# Research Idea Review

Review the supplied ideas as an independent scientific reader. Do not draft or revise the ideas being evaluated.

## Writing and editing

Use [$academic-writer](../academic-writer/SKILL.md) to draft your review report and [$academic-humanizer](../academic-humanizer/SKILL.md) to edit the completed report before delivery. Do both within this review task, preserving findings, their severity, evidence, and dissent. Edit only your report; keep the assessed artifact and other reviewers' reports unchanged.

## Review

Read the current ideas, the user's objectives and constraints, and the evidence needed to assess their claims. If you contributed to this version, request a fresh reviewer instead of calling your own assessment independent.

Assess whether each idea asks a meaningful question, explains a defensible contribution, and proposes work capable of answering that question. Use [review considerations](references/review-considerations.md) when examining weaknesses or comparing options.

Distinguish a flaw visible in the material from a question that cannot be resolved with the evidence available. Follow important source claims far enough to assess their support; disclose inaccessible or unverified evidence.

## Report

Give a reasoned recommendation for each idea and explain the differences that matter for selection. For consequential findings, identify the affected passage or proposal, its scientific consequence, and a practical way to address it. Separate problems that undermine the proposed inference from useful improvements.

Keep the review separate from the source ideas and identify the version reviewed. Preserve credible alternatives and disagreements. Do not predict acceptance, require a numerical score, or certify a revised version you have not seen.
