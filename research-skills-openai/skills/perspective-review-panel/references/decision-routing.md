# Decision Routing

Use the same route labels as orchestrator panel routing.

- `strong_support`: no substantive must-fix issues remain.
- `support_with_minor_revision`: only editorial or paragraph-local clarity issues remain.
- `support_after_major_revision`: one or more substantive but fixable issues remain.
- `not_ready`: draft cannot be repaired locally but thesis may remain viable.
- `reject_or_redesign`: thesis, evidence base, or outlet fit is not salvageable.

Severity levels:
- fatal: stop or redesign
- major: route to STEP 7 scientific revision; use STEP 4 architect when the argument structure itself is invalid
- minor: route to STEP 8.5
- editorial: route to STEP 8.5, must not block finalization once patched

The default recommendation uses the counter-position and evidence reviews. Optional
target-reader/outlet simulation is advisory and routes unresolved reader or outlet
observations to the later narrative assessment or concrete journal-matching stage; it
does not declare readiness.
