# Tension Taxonomy

Use one or more tension types to make the Perspective worth writing.

- Evidence-practice gap: evidence exists but practice has not shifted.
- Assumption-evidence mismatch: common belief exceeds or contradicts evidence.
- Mechanism-outcome gap: plausible mechanism does not translate into outcomes.
- Scale/context mismatch: findings are overextended beyond population, setting, or system.
- Methodological blind spot: common methods hide the key question.
- Policy-implementation gap: policy ambition exceeds operational reality.
- Ethical tradeoff: plausible progress creates under-addressed harms or duties.
- Timing tension: new evidence, technology, or policy makes an old issue newly urgent.

If no tension can be stated in one sentence, route back to input-builder for clarification.
