# Claim Type Taxonomy

- Empirical: states what evidence shows about the world.
- Conceptual: defines, reframes, or distinguishes ideas.
- Normative: states what should matter or what obligation follows.
- Translational: links evidence to practice or implementation.
- Policy: recommends governance, policy, or institutional action.
- Methodological: critiques or recommends methods.

Rules:
- Empirical claims need direct or adjacent evidence unless explicitly weak/speculative.
- Normative claims need stated values and boundaries.
- Translational and policy claims need feasibility or implementation limits.
- Conceptual claims still need discourse baseline support; they are not evidence-free.
