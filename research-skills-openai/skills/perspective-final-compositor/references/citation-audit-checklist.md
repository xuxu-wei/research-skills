# Citation Audit Checklist

- Every citation maps to a Claim ID or a clearly illustrative use.
- No citation is used to support a stronger claim than permitted by the matrix.
- `citation-risk-log.md` high-risk items are resolved or carried into readiness risks.
- Reference list entries include DOI/PMID/URL/user-material marker when available.
- No unverified source is used for a central claim without a warning.
- Contrary evidence is not hidden by wording polish.
- Citation style is consistent with target outlet profile.
