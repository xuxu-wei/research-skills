# Unresolved Issues Policy

## Purpose
Ensure that remaining issues are preserved clearly in the final package.

## Categories
- Blocking issue: prevents responsible submission or execution.
- Major risk: does not necessarily block submission but may draw serious review criticism.
- Minor issue: should be corrected but does not threaten the core proposal.
- Reviewer dissent: material disagreement between reviewers.
- Skeptical objection: high-value objection raised by the skeptical reviewer.
- User-confirmation-needed item: requires the user to provide or confirm information.
- Human expert review needed item: requires PI, statistician, domain expert, IRB, or other specialist review.

## Rules
- Preserve issue source when possible.
- Do not downgrade blocking issues without upstream evaluation support.
- Do not remove an issue unless an upstream revision delta or evaluation report confirms it was resolved.
- Keep skeptical objections visible even when most reviewers are supportive.
