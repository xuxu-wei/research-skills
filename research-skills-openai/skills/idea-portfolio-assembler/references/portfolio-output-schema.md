# Portfolio Output Schema

Include:

- executive navigation summary and route profile;
- one entry per Idea with title, current dossier artifact ID/version/path,
  relative dossier link, evaluation link, candidate journal-match link and
  medical journal review link when applicable, explicit non-applicability
  otherwise, narrative/language readiness links, preservation link when
  applicable, reference-ledger link, status,
  fatal/blocking findings, dissent, unresolved editorial issues, other
  unresolved issues, and next human action;
- sealed evaluator score/gate/status comparison plus separate unscored journal
  match and medical-review decision/status without rescore or outlet ranking;
- subordinate lineage and rejected/backup summary when applicable; and
- focused Proposal-handoff status or bounded human-selection state.

The portfolio is navigation, not another Idea body. Do not copy or rewrite
dossier sections. Use `no_promoted_idea_report` or
`portfolio_assembly_failure_report` when required.
