# Iterative Literature Search

Use this pattern when the topic is broad or when evidence is not supplied.

## Round 1 — Breadth

Cover different terms, populations, systems, methods, outcomes, and neighboring fields.

Goal: identify terminology, major clusters, reviews, guidelines, and active debates.

## Round 2 — Depth

Trace references, citations, related papers, author continuations, updated guidelines, and newer reviews.

Goal: verify whether apparent gaps are real or already addressed.

## Round 3 — Targeted Gap Filling

Search for guideline alignment, negative results, implementation failures, benchmark or metric limitations, data limitations, and recent frontier studies.

## Stop Conditions

Stop when search results are mostly duplicates, evidence is sufficient, sources are inaccessible and must be marked as limitations, or the orchestrator/user restricts scope.
