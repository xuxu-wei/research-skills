# Results Organization Modes

- `norm_driven`: follows standard reporting order for the study type.
- `argument_driven`: orders results by the central contribution and claim sequence.
- `hybrid`: standard order with selected argument-driven emphasis.
- `artifact_driven`: organized around a tool, dataset, model, or resource.
- `theory_driven`: ordered by theoretical constructs or mechanisms.
- `evidence_synthesis_driven`: ordered by review question, evidence class, or synthesis layer.

Choose one mode before drafting. Record the rationale and how each Results paragraph maps to claims and display items.
