# Stable Perspective Evaluation Rubric

Use this rubric for scientific and final evaluation. Score each dimension from 1 to 5
and cite the frozen Perspective paragraph or section that supports the score. In final
evaluation, use only the final Perspective and clean minimal evidence/outlet facts.

1. **Thesis clarity:** a target reader can state the central judgment and its scope.
2. **Argument integrity:** on-page reasoning advances through necessary steps without
   hidden premises, circularity, or unresolved jumps.
3. **Evidence-claim match:** material claims stay within the verified proposition,
   source intent, directness, allowed claim strength/use, locator, forbidden-use, and
   contrary-evidence limits in the clean facts.
4. **Narrative coherence:** each paragraph advances the Perspective rather than a
   miniature review or procedural commentary.
5. **Stance calibration:** confidence, counterarguments, and boundaries match the
   evidence without erasing the contribution.
6. **Contribution sufficiency:** the article changes what the declared reader should
   understand, prioritize, or question.
7. **Audience and outlet fit:** concept burden, genre, structure, length, and stance
   fit the clean outlet facts.
8. **Novelty against discourse:** the differentiating contribution is supported by
   the supplied discourse facts and does not claim absence from an incomplete search.

When clean discourse facts are `incomplete` or `unavailable`, label the novelty score
provisional, state the factual limitation, and never infer an empty discourse. Only
`verified_bounded` facts support a non-provisional judgment, and only within their
recorded verification scope.

Hard gates before scientific qualification or final acceptance: thesis clarity,
argument integrity, evidence-claim match, and contribution sufficiency must each score
at least 4; no unsupported central claim or fatal flaw may remain.

Decision labels are `accept`, `minor_revision`, `major_revision_draft`,
`argument_rebuild`, `thesis_redesign`, `evidence_rebuild`, `outlet_retarget`, and
`reject_not_salvageable`. A final evaluator does not assess compliance with a hidden
plan, ledger, map, repair brief, readiness report, or prior review.
