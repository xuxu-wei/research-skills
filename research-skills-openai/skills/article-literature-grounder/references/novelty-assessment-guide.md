# Novelty Assessment Guide

## Gap Types

- `evidence_gap`: limited or inconsistent prior evidence.
- `methodological_gap`: prior work used weaker or incompatible methods.
- `population_gap`: target sample or setting is underrepresented.
- `replication`: confirms previous findings in a new sample or setting.
- `extension`: adds endpoint, subgroup, method, or follow-up.
- `confirmation`: strengthens existing evidence without changing the question.
- `refutation`: challenges a prior assumption or result.

## Confidence Rules

- `high`: similar prior studies checked, competing evidence reviewed, and contribution remains distinct.
- `medium`: relevant literature checked but coverage is partial.
- `low`: sparse search, unverified references, or likely missing adjacent work.

Avoid "first", "novel", or "unique" unless the search can support it.
