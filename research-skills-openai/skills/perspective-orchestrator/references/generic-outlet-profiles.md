# Generic Outlet Profiles

当用户未指定目标期刊时使用。每个 Profile 提供完整的 outlet-fit 评价基准。

---

## Generic Clinical Viewpoint

- Expected reader: clinically literate, not necessarily subspecialist
- Typical length: 1200–2000 words
- Reference density: selective, high-yield only (~10–20 refs)
- Preferred structure: clinical tension → evidence gap → practical implication → boundary
- Acceptable stance strength: clear position but cautious (avoid guideline-like overreach)
- Common anti-patterns: anecdote-driven claims, mini-review drift, overgeneralization from single cases
- Title style: declarative, conveys position (not descriptive)
- Abstract requirement: typically not required; if present, ~150 words
- Figure/Box expectation: 0–1 illustrative figure or summary box
- Tone: authoritative but not polemical; acknowledges uncertainty where real

## Generic Biomedical Perspective

- Expected reader: broad biomedical research community (mixed clinical + basic)
- Typical length: 2000–4000 words
- Reference density: moderate (~20–40 refs)
- Preferred structure: opening tension → current framework limits → proposed reframing → evidence → implications → future agenda
- Acceptable stance strength: judgment-driven, evidence-calibrated
- Common anti-patterns: overclaiming from single studies, ignoring contrary evidence, mini-review drift
- Title style: conveys core thesis, not just topic
- Abstract requirement: 150–250 words
- Figure/Box expectation: 1–2 figures or summary boxes

## Generic Scientific Perspective

- Expected reader: domain scientists, methodologically literate
- Typical length: 3000–5000 words
- Reference density: moderate-heavy (~30–50 refs)
- Preferred structure: field tension → conceptual gap → proposed framework → evidence synthesis → boundary conditions → research agenda
- Acceptable stance strength: argument-driven with clear boundary marking
- Common anti-patterns: framework without evidence, overgeneralization, claim-evidence mismatch
- Title style: thesis-driven, signals contribution type
- Abstract requirement: 150–250 words
- Figure/Box expectation: 1–3 conceptual figures or summary boxes

## Generic Policy Commentary

- Expected reader: policymakers, regulators, research administrators
- Typical length: 1500–2500 words
- Reference density: selective, policy-relevant (~10–20 refs)
- Preferred structure: policy tension → current approach limits → evidence-informed alternative → implementation considerations → trade-offs
- Acceptable stance strength: actionable but hedged where evidence thin
- Common anti-patterns: overpromising, ignoring implementation barriers, conflating evidence levels
- Title style: actionable, signals policy implication
- Abstract requirement: 100–150 words or executive summary
- Figure/Box expectation: 0–1 summary box or decision framework

## Generic Methods Perspective

- Expected reader: methodologists, quantitative scientists, research designers
- Typical length: 3000–5000 words
- Reference density: thorough (~30–50 refs, methods-focused)
- Preferred structure: methodological tension → current standard limitations → proposed approach → comparative analysis → applicability → caveats
- Acceptable stance strength: technically precise, comparative rather than absolute claims
- Common anti-patterns: overclaiming generalizability, ignoring computational/practical constraints
- Title style: method-focused, signals comparative advantage
- Abstract requirement: 200–300 words
- Figure/Box expectation: 1–3 technical figures or comparison tables

---

## Usage

使用 Generic Profile 时:
- Outlet-fit 评分标注为 `provisional`
- Final-compositor 不得声明 `ready for human sign-off`，只声明 `ready for outlet targeting`
- Panel Narrative Reviewer 使用 Generic Profile 判断基本可读性
