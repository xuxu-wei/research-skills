# Statistical Audit Guide

## Core Questions

- Does the primary analysis match the design and endpoint?
- Are assumptions checked or limitations stated?
- Are effect estimates and uncertainty intervals reported?
- Is multiplicity handled when multiple primary or confirmatory tests exist?
- Are sensitivity analyses adequate for the main inference?
- Are subgroup analyses pre-specified or clearly exploratory?

## Blocking Findings

- Analysis cannot answer the primary question.
- Required reanalysis is needed before any responsible claim can be written.
- Primary result is internally inconsistent with tables/figures.
- Causal language is planned from an uncontrolled observational design without support.
