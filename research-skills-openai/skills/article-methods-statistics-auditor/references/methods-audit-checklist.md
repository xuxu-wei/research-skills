# Methods Audit Checklist

## Universal Checks

- Research question matches design.
- Primary endpoint/outcome is defined.
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria are stated.
- Sample size basis is described or limitation is acknowledged.
- Missing data handling is described.
- Analysis plan matches the stated endpoint and design.

## Design-Specific Checks

- RCT: randomization, allocation concealment, blinding, protocol deviations, trial registration.
- Observational: confounding control, selection bias, temporality, exposure/outcome measurement.
- Diagnostic: reference standard, blinding, indeterminate results, participant flow.
- Prediction model: development/validation split, calibration, discrimination, overfitting controls.
- Systematic review: protocol, search strategy, screening, risk of bias, synthesis method.
- AI/ML: train/validation/test leakage, external validation, calibration, deployment setting.
