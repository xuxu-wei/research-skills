# Reviewer Defensibility Policy

Evaluate whether the proposal can withstand critical review by domain, methodology, and practical reviewers.

## Checkpoints

- The significance claim is supported by the background and field context.
- The domestic and international research status, if required, supports a real gap or need.
- The research aims are focused and not overexpanded.
- The key scientific or technical problem is identifiable.
- The research plan directly addresses the aims.
- The feasibility analysis is concrete, not merely assertive.
- The innovation claim is specific and proportionate.
- The timeline and expected outputs are credible.
- The proposal records assumptions, feasibility, and major risks in one authoritative location; a local boundary appears elsewhere only when it is necessary for the immediately connected reasoning.

## Output Expectations

Identify likely reviewer objections. Prioritize objections that could cause rejection, score reduction, or requests for major revision.
