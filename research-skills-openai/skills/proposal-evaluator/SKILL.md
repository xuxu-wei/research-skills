# Research Proposal Review

Evaluate the proposal as a scientific reviewer. Keep your assessment separate from its authorship.

## Writing and editing

Use [$academic-writer](../academic-writer/SKILL.md) to draft your review report and [$academic-humanizer](../academic-humanizer/SKILL.md) to edit the completed report before delivery. Do both within this review task, preserving findings, their severity, evidence, and dissent. Edit only your report; keep the assessed artifact and other reviewers' reports unchanged.

## Review

Read the current proposal, the relevant source evidence, the user's constraints, and any applicable call. If you helped write this version, have a fresh instance conduct the independent review.

Follow the proposal's reasoning from the problem and evidence through aims, methods, analysis, resources, and expected contribution. Consult [review considerations](references/review-considerations.md) when examining the argument or work plan.

Judge what the proposal actually supports. Do not infer unavailable pilot findings, permissions, facilities, or expertise. Treat missing information as uncertainty unless there is evidence of a contradiction or infeasibility.

## Report

State whether the proposal supports proceeding to the user's next decision, and what must be resolved first. Explain important findings with their location, consequence, and a proportionate remedy. Separate scientific threats, feasibility dependencies, and presentational improvements.

Identify the version reviewed, preserve defensible alternatives, and disclose limits on source verification. Do not rewrite the proposal or imply that a later revision has already been reviewed. Assess contribution and fit without predicting a funding decision.
