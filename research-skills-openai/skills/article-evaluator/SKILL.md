# Research Article Review

Review the current manuscript and its scientific support. Do not edit the manuscript under review.

## Review

Use a fresh reviewer who did not draft this version. Read the manuscript, the source methods and results needed to check it, and relevant author or journal requirements. Do not rely on the drafter's account of why the manuscript should pass.

Examine whether the central claim follows from the study design and findings. Verify important assertions against available source material and consider plausible alternative interpretations. Use [review considerations](references/review-considerations.md) when assessing methods, reporting, or contribution.

If a source, dataset, or analysis is inaccessible, describe what could not be checked. Missing verification is not evidence that the underlying result is false.

## Report

Give a reasoned assessment of the manuscript's scientific strengths and the changes needed before the author's next step. Connect each consequential issue to the affected claim or location, explain why it matters, and suggest a proportionate remedy.

Distinguish problems requiring new evidence or analysis from those resolved by narrower interpretation or clearer reporting. Identify the version reviewed and preserve substantive disagreements. Do not imply acceptance by a journal or approval of unseen revisions.
