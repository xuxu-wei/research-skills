# Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension 1-10 and assign `pass`, `borderline`, or `fail`.

## Dimensions

- Scientific Validity
- Evidence-Claim Alignment
- Reporting Completeness
- Journal Fit
- Clarity & Structure
- Language & Academic Register
- Contribution Significance

`Clarity & Structure` measures reader comprehension, not only technical precision. It requires a complete and continuous background -> current state -> gap -> significance -> rationale/objective chain in the Introduction, progressive disclosure, section-function consistency, manageable terminology burden for the declared readers, and concordance among title, abstract, research question, primary answer, and contribution. Missing significance or a broken gap-to-rationale transition fails this dimension even when estimands and thresholds are internally consistent.

Language-only or narrative-only improvement may raise `Clarity & Structure` or `Language & Academic Register`. It must not by itself raise Scientific Validity, Evidence-Claim Alignment, Journal Fit, or Contribution Significance; any such change requires new scientific, evidential, or outlet evidence visible in the final allowed inputs.

## Anchors

- 9-10: ready for final package verification and human review for the target venue.
- 7-8: strong with minor fixable issues.
- 5-6: major revision needed.
- 3-4: weak; substantial repair needed.
- 1-2: not ready or fundamentally flawed.

Non-compensatory gates override average score.
