# Novelty and Repositioning Claim Rules

- Use `supported`, `plausible`, `unverified`, or `speculative` for novelty.
- Do not claim first/novel/unprecedented without direct map support.
- Similar prior work does not automatically require new work. A supported
  title/audience change may increase visibility or honestly emphasize
  validation, replication, application, translation, integration, resource, or
  benchmark value.
- If claiming scientific, data, or method novelty, identify the actual increment
  in an evidence-chain input, transformation, or output.
- Every title/positioning claim must be `supported` or retain the qualifier from
  a `qualified` Claim-Support row. Remove `unsupported` claims.
- Carry evidence limitations into the dossier using normal prose and citations,
  never unexplained internal IDs.
