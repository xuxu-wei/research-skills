# Divergent Search Routing

Apply this overlay when `mode: divergent`. Keep the surface boundary from
`search-routing-rules.md`. A genuinely multi-stage, multi-direction,
multi-source exploration requires Deep Research; a bounded Built-in Search may
only establish whether that broader route is warranted.

## Define breadth before retrieval

Record the central topic, downstream decision, source classes, time/language/
geography bounds, adjacent fields allowed, maximum exploration breadth, and stop
condition. Divergence expands evidence discovery; it does not authorize Idea
generation, ranking, or proposal drafting.

## Build distinct search lanes

Use only lanes relevant to the question:

- direct evidence and established reviews;
- contrary findings, null results, failures, and boundary conditions;
- alternative methods, measurements, datasets, or implementation settings;
- emerging evidence and recent shifts;
- adjacent fields with a documented structural analogy.

For each adjacent-field lane, state the transfer rationale: shared mechanism,
problem structure, data structure, measurement problem, or method. Superficial
topic similarity is insufficient.

## Retrieval discipline

1. Map the direct landscape before expanding.
2. Search each approved lane with distinct queries and source classes.
3. Open the sources that materially support a claim or opportunity.
4. Separate verified direct evidence from analogy and exploratory inference.
5. Run one targeted follow-up for consequential contradictions or missing source
   classes.
6. Stop when new lanes repeat known evidence, lack a defensible transfer
   rationale, or exceed the declared breadth.

Do not convert source volume into confidence. Early signals, preprints,
conference abstracts, position papers, and cross-domain analogies may be useful
discovery inputs, but retain their actual limitations.

## Claim and opportunity handling

- Assign every material claim one `support_status` from the Evidence Map schema.
- Label analogy or transfer claims `weak`, `single-source`, or `unverified`
  unless directly corroborated in the target domain.
- Preserve contradictory lanes and failed or negative searches.
- Do not fill a target number of opportunities or directions.
- Do not claim novelty from search absence or inaccessible sources.

## Handoff

Report lanes explored, adjacency rationales, sources opened, claim statuses,
conflicts, access limits, negative searches, emerging signals, and the reason for
stopping. For Idea work, return evidence-grounded direction signals only; the
orchestrator owns route selection and the writer owns dossier content.
