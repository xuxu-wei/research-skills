## Key Claims

| Claim ID | Human-readable label and atomic claim | Type | Support status | Direct-support bindings (1–5) | Direct-contradiction bindings (0–5) | Confidence | Novelty verification | Guideline alignment | Limitations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use only `supported`, `weak`, `conflicting`, `single-source`, `unverified`, or
`access-limited` for Support status. Write each binding as a separately linked
reference ID plus its claim-specific locator and source role. Keep Confidence
as a separate judgment. A scientifically supported claim has one to five
direct-support bindings: use `single-source` for one and `supported` for two to
five concordant sources. Zero direct sources cannot be marked supported, and a
sixth requires representative-source selection or claim
splitting. Keep background sources and negative-search results outside the
direct-support count.

## Deep Research Return Assessment

Omit this section when Deep Research was not used. Otherwise record the report
logical reference; acceptance decision; main-question and subquestion coverage;
claim/source traceability; citation/link completeness; closest-work and contrary
evidence coverage; applicability bounds; novelty usability; and unresolved items.

When the report is not accepted, also record repairability, the selected
lower-cost repair route, repairs already attempted, and whether an owner-approved
second Deep Research run is scientifically necessary.
