# Deep Research Follow-up Guide

The request file is ready to send without editing.

1. Open `deep-research-request-vNNN.md` and send its complete contents to a new
   Deep Research conversation. Do not append this guide or local workflow notes.
2. If the research service asks a scientific clarification that changes scope,
   answer it in that conversation and preserve the clarified scope in the
   returned report.
3. Save the complete returned report as `deep-research-report-vNNN.md` in this
   same round directory. Do not overwrite the request or guide.
4. Confirm that the report includes GB/T 7714—2015 references with complete
   links, covers the main and subquestions, closest work, contrary evidence,
   conflicts, access limits, and applicability boundaries.
5. Return the report path to the originating workflow. The landscape mapper
   will accept it, request a revision, or identify one bounded supplemental
   search. Do not resume downstream writing or evaluation before that decision.

If the originating workflow cannot see the Deep Research conversation, provide
its deep link together with the saved report path.
