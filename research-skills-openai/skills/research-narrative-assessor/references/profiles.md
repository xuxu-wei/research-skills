# Narrative profiles

Select one profile from the artifact's actual contract. Do not use profile labels to
impose sections that the artifact type does not require.

## Common handoff

Require declared readers, assumed prior knowledge, the intended reader shift, the
artifact's core question or thesis, and any contract-required sections. Missing or
conflicting handoff facts that prevent a reliable target require
`clarification_required`.

## Idea

Reader route: background -> current state -> scientific gap -> significance -> design
rationale. Keep the research question, objectives, proposed work, expected outputs,
and contribution aligned. Method and validation detail follow the reader-facing core
unless needed to identify the question. Use the artifact's designated Assumptions and
Limitations section as the single complete authority for limitation families.

## Proposal

Reader route: decision or funding need -> current state and gap -> objective ->
approach -> feasibility -> expected value. Each required section retains its distinct
decision function; avoid repeating the full approach as significance or impact.
Record complete working assumptions, feasibility conditions, and limitation/risk
families once in the proposal contract's designated authoritative assumptions,
feasibility, and risks location.

## Perspective

Reader route: field tension -> thesis -> ordered argument steps -> strongest
counterarguments and boundaries -> implications. Every paragraph should advance the
thesis rather than accumulate background. Assign one authority location to each
distinct counterargument or boundary family. Outside that family's authority, omit
the material unless a self-contained local boundary directly advances the immediately
connected argument and omission would distort it. Never insert a pointer to the
authority location.

## Article

Reader route: the Introduction establishes background -> current state -> unresolved
gap -> significance -> rationale/objective; Methods explain the design that answers
that objective; Results lead with the primary answer; Discussion begins with that
answer, interprets it, and reaches a bounded conclusion. Keep title, abstract, aims,
methods, results, and claims aligned. Complete limitation families belong in the
article's authoritative limitations location; a necessary local boundary is retained
only when it directly governs the adjacent result or interpretation.
