# Cover Letter Template

Dear [Editor Name],

We are pleased to submit "[Manuscript Title]" for consideration as a [Article Type] in [Journal].

[Problem positioning: In 2-3 sentences, state what the field already knows, the specific unresolved issue, and why that issue matters now.]

[Editorial delta: In 1-2 paragraphs, explain what the manuscript changes. Do not repeat the abstract. State whether the contribution reframes the problem, strengthens inference, clarifies a decision, enables measurement, defines boundaries, reorganizes evidence, or enables future research.]

[Journal fit: In 2-4 sentences, explain why this delta is specifically relevant to the journal's readership and editorial scope.]

[Credibility and disclosures: In 1 paragraph, include relevant prior work continuity, evidence boundaries, preprint/related manuscripts, ethics approval, conflicts, data/code availability, author approval, reviewer suggestions/exclusions if applicable, and confirmation that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.]

Sincerely,

[Corresponding Author Name]
