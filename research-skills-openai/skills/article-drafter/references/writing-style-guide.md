# Writing Style Guide

- Use precise academic prose; avoid promotional claims.
- Keep Results descriptive and move interpretation to Discussion.
- Report estimates with uncertainty intervals where available.
- Define abbreviations on first use.
- Use standard field terminology when available; otherwise prefer plain descriptive language accessible to the declared readers. Introduce a concept before dense terminology and avoid internal workflow metaphors.
- Keep the article's positive question, answer, and contribution ahead of methodological defense. Consolidate the complete limitations account in its authoritative location and omit repetition under the narrow reasoning exception in the main contract.
- Delete, merge, move, and reorder when needed; revision is not limited to adding caveats or sentences.
- Use cautious verbs for exploratory or observational findings.
- Do not introduce claims absent from the blueprint.
- Do not include reviewer-response language in manuscript body.
