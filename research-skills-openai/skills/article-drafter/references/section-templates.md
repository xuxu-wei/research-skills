# Section Templates

## Default IMRaD Order

Draft Methods, Results, Introduction, then Discussion.

## Methods Minimum

Study design; setting; participants/samples; intervention/exposure; outcome definitions; statistical analysis; reporting standard notes.

## Results Minimum

Participant/sample flow; descriptive foundation; primary result; secondary results; sensitivity/robustness; adverse events or negative findings when applicable.

## Introduction Functions

Background; current state of knowledge/evidence; the unanswered gap; why resolving it matters; study rationale and objective/hypothesis. These functions may be concise and need not be separate headings, but each must be present and connected in this order.

## Discussion Minimum

Direct answer and principal finding; comparison with literature; interpretation; implications; one authoritative strengths/limitations account; conclusion. Do not open with a defensive list of what the study cannot establish.

Follow the study-specific reporting standard selected in the context brief.
