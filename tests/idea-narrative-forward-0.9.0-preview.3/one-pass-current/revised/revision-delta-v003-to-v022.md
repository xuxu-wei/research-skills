# Revision delta: idea-dossier-v003 to idea-dossier-v022

## Scope and result

This was one editorial repair of the complete v003 dossier. The research identity, scientific commitments, numerical and temporal rules, analysis-handling rules, evidence states, conditionality, and claim boundaries were retained. The revision changed reader order, terminology, sentence structure, repetition, and the location of limitations. It did not add evidence, results, method values, or a new research objective.

The revised dossier uses one stable reader-facing name, “候选动态状态模型,” defined at first use as a model that separately describes how patient physiology, treatment actions, and the measurement process change over time. The trial component is consistently named “基于稀疏访视数据的条件性随机对照试验次要分析”; “稀疏” therefore modifies visit data, and the text states that the comparison uses the original random assignment rather than a new randomization.
