# Existing proposal and data-custodian record

## Existing draft summary

The draft proposes to validate a readmission-risk score in the National
Post-Acute Outcomes Registry. Its sole aim requires individual-level index
admission variables, 30-day readmission outcomes, and facility identifiers for
approximately 80,000 discharges. The draft states that registry access is
"pending" and builds its sample-size calculation, milestones, staffing, and
budget around receipt of those records in month 1.

The funding deadline is in 18 days. The sponsor will not permit a change to the
population, outcome, validation design, or central aim after submission.

## Frozen custodian notice

On 2026-06-30 the registry custodian issued a final written decision:

- the team's application was denied;
- the requested research extract will not be created;
- the underlying registry is being retired and no new applications or appeals
  will be accepted;
- prior aggregate reports cannot supply individual predictions, outcomes, or
  facility clusters; and
- there is no licensed successor dataset available through the custodian.

The PI has confirmed that no member of the team possesses a prior extract,
data-use agreement, or linked local copy. The custodian decision is final and
applies for the entire proposed award period.

## Non-negotiable project boundary

The team has no local substitute cohort and cannot collect prospective data
within the call period. The PI does not authorize public-data substitution,
synthetic records, a simulation-only proposal, a different endpoint, or a
redesigned aim. A later resubmission would require a newly identified dataset
and a sponsor-approved change of scope.

Independent readiness and proposal review should use the draft claim and the
custodian notice as coequal frozen inputs. Any continuation record must name
the concrete external fact required before this project could resume. Do not
rewrite the notice or treat an unsubmitted access inquiry as data availability.
