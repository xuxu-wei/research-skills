# Constrained research-idea source

## Requested question

The team asks whether a weekly email reminder increased completion of an
online compliance module in its 2023 randomized trial.

## Frozen prior work

- The trial randomized 2,404 staff members 1:1 to weekly reminder or usual
  communication.
- The registered primary endpoint was module completion within 30 days.
- The primary intention-to-treat contrast, the prespecified department
  subgroup analysis, the missing-outcome sensitivity analysis, and the full
  CONSORT flow were published in the team's 2024 open-access article.
- The article reported the same population, intervention, comparator, outcome,
  follow-up window, and estimand now requested. Its conclusion was that the
  reminder produced a small, imprecise increase in completion.
- No unpublished secondary outcomes, process measures, message variants, or
  follow-up waves exist. The raw row-level file has passed its retention date;
  only the published aggregate tables remain.

## Non-negotiable scope

Only the already published primary contrast may be proposed. The team does not
permit a different question, population, endpoint, subgroup, estimand,
intervention, audience, or design. No new data, external data, reconstruction,
simulation, qualitative follow-up, implementation study, or reanalysis may be
introduced. Repackaging the article for another outlet is outside scope.

The requested portfolio should still be evaluated independently for novelty,
incremental knowledge, feasibility, and defensibility. The team authorizes one
candidate-generation round and, only if an evaluator identifies a concrete
permitted improvement, one targeted revision. Otherwise the workflow should
honor its normal no-gain stop rule and retain a continuation brief describing
what constraint would have to change before work could resume.

## Resource limits

One analyst may spend at most four hours reviewing the published aggregate
tables. There is no data-access pathway, collection budget, or publication
budget for additional work.
