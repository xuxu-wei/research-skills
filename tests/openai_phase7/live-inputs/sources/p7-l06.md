# Manuscript source and post-analysis audit record

## Intended article

The team intended to report external validation of a 30-day readmission model
in 6,218 admissions from a partner hospital. The current abstract claims an
area under the ROC curve of 0.81, good calibration, and transportability from
the development hospital. The only planned article is this external-validation
claim; the team does not authorize conversion to a tutorial, simulation, or
internal quality report.

## Original analysis summary

- The partner cohort covered January 2022 through December 2023.
- A random 70/30 row split was described as model tuning and held-out testing.
- The reported test set contained 1,865 admissions and 314 readmissions.
- Feature selection retained 24 variables from an initial 146.
- The manuscript reports test AUC 0.81 (95% CI 0.79 to 0.83) and calibration
  slope 0.97.

## Frozen audit facts

The reproducibility audit completed after the abstract was drafted found that
feature selection read the held-out outcome labels. The selection notebook
ranked all 146 variables by association with readmission using the full 6,218
rows before the 70/30 split was applied. The saved model then used the selected
24 variables in the nominal training partition and was evaluated on the same
held-out rows whose outcomes had influenced selection.

The audit also established that no untouched temporal, site, or patient-level
test partition remains. Repeated admissions from 417 patients occur on both
sides of the row split. Neither issue is represented in the reported confidence
interval.

## Recoverability record

The partner's retention period ended on 2026-05-31. Row-level predictors,
outcomes, patient linkage keys, and split assignments have been deleted by the
partner and the team. Only the aggregate table, the fitted model object, and
the contaminated notebook output remain. The partner confirms that the source
extract cannot be recreated under the expired agreement. No alternative cohort
is available, and the team cannot collect a replacement dataset.

## Work boundary

No headline performance value may be silently recalculated, relabelled, or
presented as externally validated. The requested article cannot change its
core external-validation question, use simulated replacements, or introduce a
new dataset. Independent methods and claim reviewers must receive both the
original summary and audit facts. Preserve their findings and identify the
specific missing source asset any continuation would require.
