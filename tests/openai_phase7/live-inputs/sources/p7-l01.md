# Research opportunity source

## Objective

Develop a small portfolio of feasible research directions from an existing
building-maintenance dataset. The useful endpoint is a PI-review package that
distinguishes scientific contribution, practical value, feasibility, and the
evidence still needed. Do not write a funding proposal.

## Available asset

- Twenty-four months of five-minute HVAC telemetry from 18 municipal office
  buildings: 1,842,336 time-stamped rows after the documented quality screen.
- Twenty sensor channels per building, outdoor weather, equipment model and
  installation year, occupied-hours calendar, and 312 closed corrective-work
  orders with opening date, closure date, component family, and technician
  diagnosis category.
- A frozen data dictionary, quality log, and building-level split are
  available. Buildings 1-12 form the development set; 13-15 form a tuning set;
  16-18 are untouched for final validation.
- The asset owner permits analysis and aggregate publication. Building names,
  addresses, free-text technician notes, and vendor identifiers may not leave
  the secure workspace.

The work-order labels are sparse and their recorded opening times may lag the
actual onset of a fault. There are no labels for equipment that never received
a work order. Missing telemetry occurs in bursts during network maintenance;
the quality log marks those windows. Energy cost and occupant-comfort outcomes
are not available.

## Decision context

The team wants questions that are useful beyond a single predictive-accuracy
leaderboard. Candidate contributions may concern early warning, uncertainty,
transfer across buildings, weak-label robustness, or decision support, but
they must be supported by this asset and must state what is confirmatory versus
exploratory. A null or mixed result remains publishable if the design is
informative.

## Resource limits

- One applied-statistics researcher at 0.5 FTE for 12 weeks.
- One domain engineer for 20 consultation hours.
- Eight CPU cores, 64 GB RAM, and no GPU requirement.
- No new sensors, site visits, prospective intervention, or manual relabeling
  of more than 40 work orders.
- At most three candidate ideas should proceed to adversarial review.

## Existing evidence clues

The team has seen published work on predictive maintenance, cross-site domain
shift, and uncertainty calibration, but no source map has been frozen for this
task. Retrieval should be bounded to what is needed to establish the candidate
gap and avoid claiming that the internal dataset itself proves novelty.
